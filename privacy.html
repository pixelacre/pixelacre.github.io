Privacy policy

Seed Vault: Island Farm · Last updated: 11 October 2026

The same policy is in the game, in your own language: tap ☰ (top right) › Menu › Privacy policy.

The short version

Seed Vault doesn't collect your personal data. There is no account, no chat and no tracking. Your farm is saved on your own device and in your own iCloud.

Your game save

Your progress is saved on this device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud (part of your Apple Account), so your farm can come along to a new iPhone. We have no server of our own and never see your save. "New Game" on the title screen erases it, also from iCloud.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple Account: Apple only tells the game what was bought, so it can be given back on a new device. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Ads you choose to watch

An ad only plays when you tap "Watch ad" or "Ad: -30m". Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as coming from a child and set to family-friendly ads only: no personalised ads, no advertising ID and no tracking across apps. To show an ad, count it and stop fraud, Google receives basic technical information such as your IP address, device type and app version. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Age check

Where the law requires it (for example in some US states), the game asks Apple for your age range, never your birthday. It stays on this device and is only used to follow that law.

Children

Seed Vault is made for everyone, including children. We don't collect personal data from anyone. With Apple's Ask to Buy, a parent approves every purchase.

Our Discord

Settings has a link to our Discord server. Discord is a separate service for ages 13 and up, with its own rules and privacy policy. The game never sends anything to Discord.

If you write to us

If you email us, we get your email address and what you write. We only use it to answer you and never share it. Our email runs on Gmail (Google). Ask us and we'll delete your messages.

Your rights

Apart from emails you send us, we don't keep personal data about you, so there's nothing else for us to look up, change or delete. For the information Google handles to show ads, see Google's privacy policy. Questions? Write to pixelacregames@gmail.com.

Who we are

Seed Vault is made by Pixel Acre. Contact: pixelacregames@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is in the game and behind the privacy policy link on the App Store page, with a new date.
