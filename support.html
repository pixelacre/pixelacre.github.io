Questions

I got a new iPhone. How do I get my farm back?

Your farm is copied to your own iCloud. On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and make sure iCloud is on. Install Seed Vault and tap Continue on the title screen, where it says "Your farm from iCloud".

How do I get my purchases back?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account. In the game, tap ☰ (top right) › Menu › Purchases › Restore. The Shop also has a Restore purchases button.

Where do I enter a gift code?

Tap ☰ (top right) › Menu › Gift code › Redeem. New codes are posted on our Discord.

How do I change the language?

Tap ☰ (top right) › Menu › Language. Seed Vault is in 13 languages: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

How do I start over?

Tap ☰ (top right) › Menu › New game › Start over, or New Game on the title screen. This erases your farm, also from iCloud, so it can't be undone. Your purchases stay with your Apple Account.

Does the game need the internet?

No, Seed Vault plays offline. Ads (only when you tap one) and Game Center need a connection.

Do I have to pay?

No. Seed Vault is free to play and you never need to buy anything to finish it. Optional purchases show their exact price and contents, and nothing in the game is random for money. With Apple's Ask to Buy, a parent approves every purchase.

What about ads?

An ad only plays when you tap "Watch ad" or "Ad: -30m". Ads are family-friendly and use no personal data. Read more in our privacy policy.

Can the game tell me when my builders are done?

Yes, if you want. Turn the messages on or off in ☰ (top right) › Menu under Builder notifications.
