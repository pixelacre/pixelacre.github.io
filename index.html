A cozy farming game about seeds

Seed Vault is a pixel-art farming game for iPhone. Plant and water through four seasons, breed crops to pass on traits like Glowing and Frost-proof, and fill the vault with over 130 varieties. Every Island Level brings new land, and the villagers come back one by one.

Grow

  • Till, plant, water and harvest through four seasons
  • Sprinklers, bee hives, a greenhouse and an Auto-Field
  • Fruit trees, animals and machines for jam, flour and cheese

Breed

  • Cross two crops to pass on traits
  • Over 130 varieties, up to ancient seeds deep in the mines
  • Master each crop to make it worth more

Build

  • Restore the bakery, the inn, the blacksmith and more
  • New land with every Island Level: a beach, a quarry, the West Woods
  • A harbour where merchant ships come with seed orders

Collect

  • Rare colour harvests for the museum
  • A new Seed Show theme every week
  • Fishing, mining, cooking, quests and daily challenges

Make it yours

  • Farmer outfits and farmhouse styles
  • Pets that follow you around, from a red panda to a baby dragon
  • Earn outfits and pets just by playing

Good to know

  • Free to play. You never need to buy anything to finish the game, and nothing in the game is random for money.
  • Ads only play when you tap "Watch ad". They are family-friendly and use no personal data.
  • Plays offline. Your farm is saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, so it comes back on a new phone.
  • In 13 languages, with Game Center leaderboards and achievements.
